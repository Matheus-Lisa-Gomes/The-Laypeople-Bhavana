## ☸ Mission & Purpose

**The Lay Dharma Household Bhāvanā** compiles the dispersed wealth of Theravāda meditation into a unified, rigorous, and accessible study aggregate.

While modern meditators often encounter sectarian disputes regarding "Samatha vs. Vipassanā", "Jhana depth", "Noting vs. Bare Awareness", or "Abhidhamma vs. Suttas", this platform brings the actual methods, lineages, masters, and canonical references side-by-side so students can study and verify their practice with discerning clarity.

## 🏛️ Traditions & Lineage Clusters

### 1. Canonical Early Buddhist Texts (EBT)
- **Ānāpānasati (MN 118)**: 16 sequential contemplations in 4 tetrads fulfilling the Four Foundations of Mindfulness and Seven Awakening Factors.
- **Cattāro Satipaṭṭhānā (MN 10, DN 22)**: The direct path (*ekāyano maggo*) across Body (*Kāya*), Feeling (*Vedanā*), Mind (*Citta*), and Phenomena (*Dhammā*).
- **Yuganaddha & The Jhānas (AN 4.170, MN 119, AN 9.36)**: Developing serenity and insight in tandem; the four material and four immaterial absorptions.

### 2. The Thai Forest Tradition (Kammaṭṭhāna)
- **Ajahn Chah (Wat Nong Pah Pong / Amaravati)**: "Still Flowing Water", natural awareness (*The One Who Knows*), *Mai Nae* ("It's not certain"), radical letting go (*cāga*) in daily work.
- **Ajahn Lee Dhammadharo (Wat Asokaram)**: Method 1 & 2 of Breath Meditation, circulating breath energy (*vāyo*) through the nervous system, spine, and brain to heal the body and access Jhāna.
- **Ajahn Brahm (Bodhinyana Monastery)**: The 7 stages to stillness, kindfulness, letting go of the doer, beautiful breath, luminous *nimitta*, and deep absorption Jhānas.
- **Ajahn Maha Boowa (Wat Pa Baan Taad)**: *Buddho* mantra, heroic effort (*viriya*), dissecting severe physical pain to discover the luminous unentangled *citta*.

### 3. Burmese Vipassanā & Samatha Lineages
- **Mahāsi Sayādaw & Sayadaw U Pandita**: Momentary concentration (*khaṇika samādhi*), abdominal rise/fall, relentless mental labeling/noting, navigating the 16 Stages of Insight Knowledge (*Visuddhiñāṇa*).
- **S.N. Goenka & Ledi Sayādaw**: Direct observation of *anicca* through systematic part-by-part body sweeping (*vedanā*), strict equanimity (*upekkhā*), experiencing dissolution (*bhaṅga*).
- **Pa-Auk Sayādaw (Classical Visuddhimagga)**: Systematic mastery of all 8 Jhānas with nostril *nimitta*, Four Elements analysis into *rūpa-kalāpas*, *nāma* mental factors, and past/future Dependent Arising.
- **Sayadaw U Tejaniya (Cittānupassanā)**: Relaxed, continuous awareness of the observing mind's attitude, investigating defilements with interest rather than aversion, seamless practice throughout 24 hours.

### 4. Sri Lankan Forest Hermitage Tradition
- **Ven. Kaṭukurunde Ñāṇananda (Meetirigala Hermitage)**: The cessation of conceptual proliferation (*papañca-nirodha*), deconstructing linguistic illusions (*paññatti*), *Suññatā* (Emptiness), *The Magic of the Mind*.

### 5. Contemporary & Sutta-Based Integrations
- **Bhante Vimalaramsi (TWIM)**: Tranquil Wisdom Insight Meditation, the 6Rs (Recognize, Release, Relax head tension, Re-smile, Return, Repeat), Mettā as a complete vehicle to Nibbāna.
- **Ven. Bhikkhu Anālayo**: Holistic Satipaṭṭhāna balancing calm and insight, comparative Pāli/Chinese Āgama scholarship.

## 🧭 The 40 Classical Kammaṭṭhānas

Full interactive database based on the *Visuddhimagga*, filterable by dominant mental temperament (*Carita*):
- 🔥 **Rāga (Greed / Passion)**: 10 Asubha foulness contemplations, 31 body parts, food repulsiveness.
- ⚡ **Dosa (Aversion / Anger)**: 4 Brahmavihāras (Loving-Kindness, Compassion, Appreciative Joy, Equanimity), 4 Color Kasiṇas (Blue, Yellow, Red, White).
- 🌫️ **Moha (Delusion) & Vitakka (Restlessness)**: Ānāpānasati (Breathing), Light Kasiṇa, White Kasiṇa.
- 💎 **Saddhā (Faith / Devotion)**: 6 Anussatis (Buddha, Dhamma, Sangha, Sila, Generosity, Devas).
- 💡 **Buddhi (Intellect / Wisdom)**: Mindfulness of Death (*Maraṇassati*), Analysis of the Four Elements, Recollection of Peace (*Upasamānussati*), Formless Attainments.

## 🗺️ The 16 Stages of Insight (Visuddhiñāṇa)

Complete roadmap tracking the progress of insight from initial discernment to awakening:
1. *Nāmarūpapariccheda-ñāṇa* (Discerning Mind & Matter)
2. *Paccayatpariggaha-ñāṇa* (Discerning Cause & Effect)
3. *Sammasana-ñāṇa* (Comprehension by Groups / Three Marks)
4. *Udayabbaya-ñāṇa* (Arising & Passing Away — with Pitfalls of Insight / *Vipassanupakkilesa*)
5. *Bhaṅga-ñāṇa* (Dissolution)
6. *Bhaya-ñāṇa* (Fearfulness)
7. *Ādīnava-ñāṇa* (Danger)
8. *Nibbidā-ñāṇa* (Disenchantment)
9. *Muñcitukamyatā-ñāṇa* (Desire for Deliverance)
10. *Paṭisaṅkhā-ñāṇa* (Re-investigation)
11. *Saṅkhārupekkhā-ñāṇa* (Equanimity towards Formations)
12. *Anuloma-ñāṇa* (Adaptation)
13. *Gotrabhu-ñāṇa* (Change of Lineage)
14. *Magga-ñāṇa* (Noble Path Knowledge)
15. *Phala-ñāṇa* (Fruition Knowledge)
16. *Paccavekkhaṇa-ñāṇa* (Reviewing Knowledge)

Emergency diagnostic and antidote toolkit with classical water similes, root conditional causes, and specific remedies from 4 traditions:

- **Byāpāda (Ill-Will / Anger)**: Boiling water simile.
- **Thīna-Middha (Sloth & Torpor)**: Algae-covered water simile.
- **Uddhacca-Kukkucca (Restlessness & Remorse)**: Wind-whipped water simile.
- **Vicikicchā (Skeptical Doubt)**: Muddy dark well simile.

## 💻 Technical Architecture & Offline Use

- **Zero Runtime Dependencies**: Pure HTML5, CSS3, and ES6+ vanilla JavaScript.
- **Universal Standalone Execution**: Pre-compiled `js/bundle.js` runs directly on `file://` by double-clicking `index.html` as well as on any HTTP server.
- **Build Script**: `build_bundle.ps1` consolidates modular data files into the standalone bundle.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start & Deployment

1. **Double-click to run**: Simply open `index.html` in any browser.
2. **Local HTTP Server**: Run `.\server.ps1` or `Start_The_Lay_Dharma.bat` to launch on `http://localhost:8080`.
3. **Rebuild bundle**: Run `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File build_bundle.ps1`.
